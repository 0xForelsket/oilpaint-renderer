== Stroke-level parallelism in the real stroke list (logs/parallelism_dag_bsp.txt) ==
conflict = overlapping footprints (segment bbox grown by the kernel's hwmax), layers are barriers
32 px cells: critical path 23.7 of 35.6 s -> ideal speed-up 1.5x with unlimited cores
   DAG list schedule: 1.45x on 2 cores, 1.50x on 16; level-synchronous (GPU-style): 1.28x / 1.32x
8 px cells:  ideal 1.7x; DAG 1.56x (2 cores), 1.65x (16); BSP 1.35x / 1.42x
big-stroke layers L3-L6: 1.1-1.2x at best (painterly sweep: consecutive strokes overlap)

== FP-flag sensitivity (1200x1500 full replay, exp/bench_flags.py) ==
-O3 -march=native -ffast-math 9.7-9.9 s; -O3 -march=x86-64-v2 -ffp-contract=off 11.3 s (contended)
strict vs fast-math images: max 124/255, 9.9% of pixels differ by > 1/255  (chaotic amplification)
cause: along-sample count ns = ceil(total_s*6)+3; planner steps are exactly 0.5 width, so total_s*6 is an integer
  +-rounding for 12,181 of 12,495 strokes; ns decides how many xorshift draws each lane consumes -> whole bristle
  pattern of the stroke changes when ns flips.
ns differs from the 600 px value for 0 strokes at 1200/2400 (power-of-two scale is exact), but for
  3484 (1000 px), 3450 (1800), 3618 (3000), 3450 (3600) of 12,495 strokes
real replay, downsampled to 600: 1200 vs 2400 mean 0.88/255, 0.5% px > 8/255; 1800 vs 2400 mean 2.85/255, 10.3% px > 8/255
